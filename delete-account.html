Delete your TennisDuel account

Last updated: 29 September 2026

In the app

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you can't open the app, email panagiwtis.iwannidis@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my TennisDuel account”. We delete the account and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Your account and sign-in, your profile and photo, your location, your likes, matches and chat messages, your challenges and match results, the match photos you uploaded, and your reports and blocks. Nothing is kept afterwards, apart from copies in our hosting provider's backups, which are overwritten shortly after.
