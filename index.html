Common questions

Why can't I see anyone nearby?
Allow location access in Settings → TennisDuel → Location, or set a location yourself in the app, and try a larger search radius in the filters.

How do I report or block someone?
Tap the ⋯ button on their card in Discover, next to their name in search, or at the top of your chat with them, and choose Report or Block. To report a single message, press and hold it. Blocked users can't find you, see you or message you, and they aren't told. You can unblock people in Profile → Settings → Blocked players.

A match result is wrong.
Ask the tournament admin to correct it, or email us with the tournament name.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Settings → Delete account. It removes your account and all of your data permanently. See the Privacy Policy for details.
