Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

TennisDuel helps tennis players find opponents nearby, arrange matches, run tournaments and keep score. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

TennisDuel is run by Panagiotis Ioannidis, an individual developer based in Greece, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: panagiwtis.iwannidis@gmail.com.

What we collect

InformationWhy
Email address, or the identifier Apple or Google gives us when you sign in with them To create your account and let you sign in.
Profile: name, photo, short bio, date of birth, playing level, style, dominant hand, years playing, racket, availability and preferred surfaces To show you to other players and match you with suitable opponents.
Location: your device's location while the app is open (only if you allow it), or a location you choose yourself To find players near you and show how far away they are.
Likes and passes on other players, matches, challenges and chat messages To connect you with other players and let you talk to them.
Match scores, tournament entries and results, and photos you attach to a match To run tournaments, keep standings and show your statistics.
When you were last active To show other players whether you are online.
Reports you make about other users, and users you block To keep the community safe and hide people you don't want to see.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not sell your information, and the app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Who can see it

Other TennisDuel users can see your profile (name, photo, bio, age, tennis details and availability), how far away you are, whether you are online, and your match and tournament results. Your chat messages are visible only to the people in that conversation. Match photos are visible only to members of that tournament. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Service providers. Our data is stored and processed by Supabase, which hosts the database, sign-in and file storage for the app. If you sign in with Apple or Google, that company handles the sign-in under its own privacy policy. Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area; where they do, they rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

Legal basis (EEA and UK users)

We process your information to provide the service you signed up for (performance of a contract). We use your device's location only with your permission (consent), which you can withdraw at any time in your phone's settings. We process reports and blocks to keep users safe (legitimate interest).

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, photos, messages, likes, matches and results are deleted from our systems straight away. Backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within a short period after that.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and everything linked to it permanently. If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Your rights

You can see and correct most of your information in the app. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by emailing panagiwtis.iwannidis@gmail.com. If you are in the EEA, you can complain to your data protection authority — in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and access to it is limited by the database's access rules so that users can only change their own data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
